The game

What's in it

  1. Fifty waves

    Ram drones, gunboats, torpedo boats, submarines, missile destroyers and battleships, with a flagship every five waves.

  2. Bigger hulls

    Start in a corvette with one gun. Refit into a destroyer, a missile cruiser and a dreadnought whose turrets train and fire where you aim.

  3. Four ways to play

    Story, Skirmish, Challenge on one life, and Arcade with upgrade cards after every wave.

  4. Offline and private

    No ads, no in-app purchases, no account. Plays offline and saves only to your phone.
