Test Holdout on iPhone

Play Holdout: The Last Fleet early through Apple's TestFlight and help us catch bugs before launch.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • Apple's free TestFlight app from the App Store.

Join the beta

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the link below on your iPhone and tap Accept, then Install.
Join the iPhone beta

Testing on iPhone is free. Test builds expire when testing ends.
