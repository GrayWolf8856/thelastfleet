The Last Fleet: Holdout

Support

Last updated: November 11, 2026

The Last Fleet: Holdout is a twin-stick naval shooter for iPhone: one ship against fifty waves of a drone fleet.

How to play

  • Steer with your left thumb. Aim and fire with your right.
  • Collect the scrap that sunk ships leave behind.
  • Between waves, spend scrap on upgrades, weapons and bigger hulls.

Frequently asked

Do I need an internet connection? No. The game plays fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases? No.

Where is my progress stored? On your device only. Deleting the game deletes it. See the Privacy Policy.

Which devices does it run on? iPhone, in landscape.
