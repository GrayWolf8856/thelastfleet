The Last Fleet: Holdout

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Developer: Kyle Cosper (Crosswake Studios), USA

The Last Fleet: Holdout ("the game") is an offline naval shooter for iPhone. Your privacy is simple to describe because the game keeps everything on your device.

The game collects no personal data. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There is no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.

Data we collect

None. The game has no account system and does not require sign-in. It contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not connect to the internet.

Where your data lives

Your progress, scores and settings are saved locally on your device. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Purchases

The game is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details. There are no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
