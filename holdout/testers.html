Test Holdout on Android

Holdout: The Last Fleet is coming to Google Play. Help test it before launch.

What you need

  • An Android phone with Android 7.0 or later.
  • The Google account you use on that phone.

Step 1: Join the tester group

Join with your Google account. We never see or store your email on this website.

Join the tester group

Step 2: Opt in on Google Play

Open this on your Android phone, signed in with the same Google account, then tap Become a tester.

Open the Google Play test page

Step 3: Install and play

Install Holdout from the Play Store link on that page and play. Keep it installed for at least 14 days. Google requires testers to stay opted in for 14 days before the game can launch.

Testing builds are a paid early-access copy at $1.99.

Feedback

Found a bug or have an idea? Email kyleecosper@gmail.com, or use the Send feedback option on the Play Store test page.

This page has no forms and collects nothing. The tester group is managed by Google, and its member list is visible only to the developer.
