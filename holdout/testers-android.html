Test Holdout on Android

Play Holdout: The Last Fleet early on Google Play and help us catch bugs before launch.

What you need

  • An Android phone with Android 7.0 or later.
  • The Google account you use on that phone.

Step 1: Join the tester group

Tap the button below. On the Google Groups page, tap Join group at the top and confirm.

Join the tester group

Only see a Sign in button? Sign in with the Google account you use on your phone, and Join group will appear. We never see or store your email on this website.

Step 2: Opt in on Google Play

Open this on your Android phone, signed in with the same Google account, then tap Become a tester. If it says you're not eligible, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

Open the Google Play test page

Step 3: Get the game and play

Get Holdout from the Play Store link on that page ($1.99, and it's yours to keep). Keep it installed for at least 14 days. Google requires testers to stay opted in for 14 days before the game can launch.

Feedback

Found a bug or have an idea? Email kyleecosper@gmail.com, or use the Send feedback option on the Play Store test page.

This page has no forms and collects nothing. The tester group is managed by Google, and its member list is visible only to the developer.
