Crosswake Studios

We make naval games, from strategy and shooters to trading and rescue, where opposing sides have understandable motives and every choice leaves consequences behind.

All of our games share one universe, The Last Fleet: a drowned Earth after a war that nobody won, where the last ships of the old navies, and the people who salvage, trade and run cargo around them, try to survive.

Artemis Protocol

A naval survival strategy game on a drowned Earth. The war is over and nobody won. You command the carrier ARTEMIS and what is left of her battle group: salvage wrecks, take oil rigs and build a new fleet from scrap, because nothing like the old ships will ever be built again.
