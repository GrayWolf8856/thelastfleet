How she works

  • Cargo: 4 × 20 ft containers on marked deck spots and 6 pallets on deck. A crane at the berth lifts cargo on and off from above; nothing rolls on.
  • Loot: sunk pirates leave pallets floating, each with a random load. A net boom on each side swings out over the water and scoops a pallet up as she drives past. It lands on one of her 6 pallet places, so a full deck takes no more. She has no magnet, so she cannot lift scrap.
  • Fishing: She does two jobs. She tows a long net (36 floats) at up to 8 m/s and hauls it about every 20 seconds, a large catch each haul, losing nearly half her speed while it is out. And she works 6 pots the same way as the Crab boat.
  • The catch: it goes down the fish-hold hatch (100 units) and shows as fish boxes and crab crates on her pallet places. At the berth a crane lifts it off.
  • Fighting: 5 weapon mounts (3 small, 2 medium). The player buys and fits the guns; each one trains and fires on its own pad.
  • Handling: 31 m/s top speed, 1400 hull points.
