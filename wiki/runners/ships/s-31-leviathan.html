How she works

  • Cargo: 20 × 20 ft containers on marked deck spots and 8 pallets on deck. A crane at the berth lifts cargo on and off from above; nothing rolls on.
  • Below deck: stores hold, 60 units. A deck hatch opens onto it and the crane works straight down through the hatch.
  • Loot: sunk pirates leave pallets floating, each with a random load. A net boom on each side swings out over the water and scoops a pallet up as she drives past. It lands on one of her 8 pallet places, so a full deck takes no more. She has no magnet, so she cannot lift scrap.
  • Fighting: 6 weapon mounts (4 small, 2 medium). The player buys and fits the guns; each one trains and fires on its own pad.
  • Handling: 28 m/s top speed, 1500 hull points.

Ideas for her

Proposals, not decided yet.
