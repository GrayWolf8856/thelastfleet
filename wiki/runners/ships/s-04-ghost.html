How she works

  • Smuggling: 16 pallets ride in the hidden hold in her hull. The way in is through her two fake containers: their tops open and a crane lowers pallets down through them. Shut, nothing shows. High income, high risk.
  • Honest cargo, in plain sight: 16 pallets on deck and 2 real 20 ft containers (8 pallets each). That is 32 pallets of honest cargo against 16 hidden. Loaded by crane.
  • Loot: sunk pirates leave pallets floating, each with a random load. A net boom on each side swings out over the water and scoops a pallet up as she drives past. It lands on one of her 16 pallet places, so a full deck takes no more. She has no magnet, so she cannot lift scrap.
  • Fighting: 3 weapon mounts (2 small, 1 medium). The player buys and fits the guns; each one trains and fires on its own pad.
  • Handling: 60 m/s top speed, 380 hull points.

Ideas for her

Proposals, not decided yet.

  • Two real, two fake: of the four containers on her deck, two are real and carry cargo, and two are fakes with tops that open. Proposal: the fakes are the after pair, side by side.
  • A crane lowers pallets down through the two fakes into the hidden hold: 16 pallets in one layer, 8 under each fake, about 1.8 m of headroom.
  • A fake container is part of the ship model. Build it to the exact size and look of the kit container, 2.44 x 2.59 x 6.06 m, with no floor, so it cannot be told from the real ones beside it. Each top is a separate, animated piece that opens to clear sky.
  • With the tops shut she looks like a small freighter carrying four ordinary boxes.
  • Everything smuggled is pallets, hidden in the hull. No smuggled goods ride in a container or in sight.
  • The hidden hold is a real, hollow space in the hull, with clear sky over its opening so a crane can lower pallets in and lift them out.
  • The way in is hidden and animated. When it is shut, nothing on deck gives it away: no handles, hinges or seams that an ordinary ship would not have.
