How she works

  • Cargo: 1 × 20 ft container on marked deck spots and 3 pallets on deck. A crane at the berth lifts cargo on and off from above; nothing rolls on.
  • Loot: sunk pirates leave pallets floating, each with a random load. A net boom on each side swings out over the water and scoops a pallet up as she drives past. It lands on one of her 3 pallet places, so a full deck takes no more. She has no magnet, so she cannot lift scrap.
  • Fishing: Under way (1 to 10 m/s) she drags a trawl astern: the two doors go over the quarters, the net streams out behind with its line of floats starting about 16 m astern, and the drum hauls it back over the stern about every 14 seconds. Trawling costs her about a third of her speed.
  • The catch: it goes down the fish-hold hatch (20 units) and shows as fish boxes and crab crates on her pallet places. At the berth a crane lifts it off.
  • Fighting: 2 weapon mounts (2 small). The player buys and fits the guns; each one trains and fires on its own pad.
  • Handling: 35 m/s top speed, 280 hull points.
