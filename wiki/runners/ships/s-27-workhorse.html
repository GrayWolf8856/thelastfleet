How she works

  • Cargo: 8 × 20 ft containers on marked deck spots and 4 pallets on deck. A crane at the berth lifts cargo on and off from above; nothing rolls on.
  • Loot: sunk pirates leave pallets floating, each with a random load. A net boom on each side swings out over the water and scoops a pallet up as she drives past. It lands on one of her 4 pallet places, so a full deck takes no more. She has no magnet, so she cannot lift scrap.
  • Fighting: 3 weapon mounts (2 small, 1 medium). The player buys and fits the guns; each one trains and fires on its own pad.
  • Handling: 34 m/s top speed, 450 hull points.
