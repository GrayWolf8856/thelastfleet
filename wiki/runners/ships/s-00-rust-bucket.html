Her look

The Scrapper run down: bent rails, the wheelhouse roof rusted through, the windows boarded up, and rust streaks and patched plating from bow to stern.

How she works

  • Starter: every player begins in her, free. Every first ship of the other lines is traded up from her.
  • Refit: at ARI's yard she can be traded in and rebuilt as a clean Scrapper for 500 CR.
  • Cargo: 3 pallets on deck. A crane at the berth lifts cargo on and off from above; nothing rolls on.
  • Scrap: her deck crane swings its magnet out over the side, lifts scrap from the water and drops it in the open bin on deck. Holds 8 units.
  • Loot: sunk pirates leave pallets floating, each with a random load. Her magnet crane lifts a pallet aboard onto one of her 3 pallet places, so a full deck takes no more.
  • Towing: none. She has no winch.
  • Fighting: 1 weapon mount (1 small). The player buys and fits the guns; each one trains and fires on its own pad.
  • Handling: 45 m/s top speed, 90 hull points. Her plating is rotten through in places, so she is a little slower and weaker than a clean Scrapper (48 m/s, 100).

From the World Atlas

Starter: the player's Scrapper is the Rust bucket, her own model: the approved Scrapper made into a boat that was due for the scrapyard: bent rails, roof rusted through, windows boarded up. Booker gives the Runner a boat nobody wanted. The clean Scrapper is the one you can buy. Because she's in bad shape the Rust bucket is slightly slower and weaker: 45 m/s and 90 hull points (clean Scrapper 48 m/s, 100).
