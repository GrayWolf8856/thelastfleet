Calling all Fleet supporters

Fleet Voices

Put your voice on the radio in Runners. When a runner pulls up beside another boat, the crew calls across the water: a fishing tip, a pirate warning, a bad joke. Record a line or two, and if your take makes it into the game, your name goes in the credits.

Record your lines Join our Discord

How it works

  1. Pick up to three lines below. You can add one line of your own too.
  2. Record each one right here on your phone or computer, or upload a recording you made.
  3. Fill in the name you want in the credits, tick the two boxes and send.

Recording tips

  • Find a quiet room. Turn off the TV and music.
  • Hold your phone about a hand's width from your mouth.
  • Say the line three times, a little differently each time, with a short pause between.
  • Sound like someone on a working boat calling across the water. No swearing, no pirate "arr".
  • Change a word or two if it sounds more natural in your voice.
  • Every accent is welcome. The fleet comes from all over.

Recording here needs your microphone. If it doesn't start (for example inside the Instagram or TikTok app), open this page in Safari or Chrome, or record a voice memo and upload it.
