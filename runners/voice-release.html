Fleet Voices release

Version v1, 19 November 2026. This is what you agree to when you send us a recording.

The short version: you let us use your recording in our games and videos, for free and for good. We can edit it and add effects like radio static. We will never use it to make an AI copy of your voice. If we use it, we credit you by the name you choose, unless you ask us not to.

"We" and "us" means Crosswake Studios, the maker of The Last Fleet games. "You" means the person sending the recording. "Recording" means every audio file you send us through the Fleet Voices page.

1. What you let us do

You give us a worldwide, royalty-free, permanent permission to use your recording, and the name you choose to be credited by, in The Last Fleet games, in their updates and new versions, and in trailers, adverts, social media posts and videos about them. That includes copying, editing, cutting, mixing, changing the speed or pitch of, and adding sound effects to your recording.

This permission is not exclusive. Your voice is still yours, and you are free to record anything for anyone else.

2. We may not use it

We pick the recordings that fit the game. Sending one does not mean it will be used, and we do not have to tell you if it is not.

3. No payment, but credit

You are not paid for your recording, now or later. If we use it in a game, we list the name you gave us in that game's credits or on this website, unless you chose not to be credited. We may shorten or leave out a name that is offensive or is someone else's.

4. No AI copies of your voice

We will not use your recording to train, build or improve any AI voice model, and we will not make your voice say anything you did not record. We will not give or sell your recording to anyone who wants to do that.

5. What you promise

  • You are 18 or older.
  • The voice in the recording is yours, and only yours.
  • You made the recording yourself, and nothing in it belongs to someone else: no music, no TV or film audio, no quotes from other games or films, and no impressions of real or famous people.
  • Nothing stops you from agreeing to this, such as a talent agency, union or work contract. If you are a member of a performers' union, it is up to you to check that its rules allow this.

6. If you change your mind

Email us and we will stop putting your recording into anything new as soon as we reasonably can. We cannot remove it from game versions or videos that are already published, so the permission in section 1 continues for those.

7. Your details

We keep the name you want credited, your email, your Discord name if you give it, your recordings and a record of what you agreed to and when. We use your email only to talk to you about your recordings and your credit. We never sell your details or add you to a mailing list. Everything is stored in our Google account.

You can ask us to delete your details at any time. While we still use your recording, we keep your name and the record that you agreed to this release.

8. Contact

Email kyleecosper@gmail.com with your submission ID (it starts with FV-) for anything about your recording.
