Fleet Voices release

Version v1, 19 November 2026. This is what you agree to when you send us a recording.

The short version: you let us use your recording in our games and videos, for free and for good. We can edit it and add effects like radio static. We will never use it to make an AI copy of your voice. If we use it, we credit you by the name you choose, unless you ask us not to.

"We" and "us" means Crosswake Studios, the maker of The Last Fleet games, and its owner. "You" means the person sending the recording. "Recording" means every audio file you send us through the Fleet Voices page. "The games" means The Last Fleet games, including Runners, and any later game or version set in The Last Fleet world.

1. What you let us do

You give us a worldwide, royalty-free, permanent permission to use your recording, and the name you choose to be credited by, in the games, in their updates and new versions, and in trailers, adverts, store pages, social media posts and videos about them. That includes copying, editing, shortening, mixing, changing the speed or pitch of, and adding sound effects to your recording.

The permission also covers the people who help us bring the games to players: app stores and game stores, publishers and partners who work on the games with us, and players who stream or post videos of themselves playing. If The Last Fleet games ever move to a company we set up or to a new owner, this permission moves with them.

This permission is not exclusive. Your voice is still yours, and you are free to record anything for anyone else.

2. We may not use it

We pick the recordings that fit the game. Sending one does not mean it will be used, and we do not have to tell you if it is not. You agree that we decide how your recording is edited and where it is used, and you do not need to approve the final version.

3. No payment, but credit

You are not paid for your recording, now or later, even if the games make money. If we use it in a game, we list the name you gave us in that game's credits or on this website, unless you chose not to be credited. We may shorten or leave out a name that is offensive or is someone else's. If we forget a credit by mistake, we fix it when you tell us. That is the only fix you can ask for.

4. No AI copies of your voice

We will not use your recording to train, build or improve any AI voice model. We will not turn your recording into a different voice, and we will not make your voice say words you did not record. We will not give or sell your recording to anyone who wants to do those things.

We may use ordinary sound tools to clean up a recording, for example to remove background noise or hum, even when those tools use AI. They only clean up what you recorded.

5. What you promise

  • You are 18 or older.
  • The voice in the recording is yours, and only yours.
  • You made the recording yourself, and nothing in it belongs to someone else: no music, no TV or film audio, no quotes from other games or films, and no impressions of real or famous people.
  • Nothing stops you from agreeing to this, such as a talent agency, union or work contract. If you are a member of a performers' union, it is up to you to check that its rules allow this.

6. If you change your mind

Email us and we will stop putting your recording into anything new as soon as we reasonably can. That is a promise we make to you; it does not cancel this release. We cannot remove your recording from game versions, trailers or videos that are already out, and the permission in section 1 continues for them, including for players who already have the game.

7. Your details

We keep your full name as you signed, the name you want credited, your email, your Discord name if you give it, your recordings and a record of what you agreed to and when. Your full name is only for this agreement. We never show it in the games or on this website. We use your email only to talk to you about your recordings and your credit. We never sell your details or add you to a mailing list. Everything is stored in our Google account in the United States.

You can ask us to delete your details at any time. While we still use your recording, we keep your name and the record that you agreed to this release.

8. Signing

When you type your full name and tick the box on the Fleet Voices page, you sign this release. It counts the same as signing it on paper.

9. The rest

  • We will not change this release for recordings you have already sent. If we change it, the new version gets a new version number and covers only recordings sent after that.
  • If one part of this release cannot be enforced, the rest still applies.
  • This release is under the laws of Texas, United States.

10. Contact

Email kyleecosper@gmail.com with your submission ID (it starts with FV-) for anything about your recording.
