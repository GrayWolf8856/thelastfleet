The story

Benny was one of ARI's best runners until he lost a cargo of medicine and the port let him go. He went to the Tollmen with everything he knew about how runners think, the routes they take and the lights they hide by. Now he hunts them with incendiary rounds and leaves their ships burning in the Cut as a warning.

How they fight

The fastest thing on the Cut. He fires incendiary rounds, so fire spreads on your deck. Put fires out fast or lose the cargo. He knows runner tricks, so the usual ones won't work.
