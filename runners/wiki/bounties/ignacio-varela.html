Wanted by the posters who suffer from The Tollmen. Number 10 of their ten.

Role

Hunting ground

Known for
Broadcasting a 'final notice' to a ship before he sinks it. Nobody has heard a second one.

The story

A banker in Mexico City when the Debt War began. He watched the world fight over money it no longer had, and he decided the debts were still real. He built the Tollmen one ledger at a time, and he believes the whole world still owes him. Take him and the Counting House, and the ledgers burn.

How they fight

Missiles from long range, a ring of escorts, and the Counting House's guns. He reads your final notice over the radio before the first missile.
