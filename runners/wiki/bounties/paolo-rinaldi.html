The story

A powerboat racer from Naples who came south when the races stopped. He likes the shallows because nobody can follow him there. He races for the joy of it; the cargo is a bonus.

How they fight

Very fast and very shallow. He'll run you onto the ruins if you chase him in a deep hull.
