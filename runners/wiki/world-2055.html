The world in 2055

The world of Runners, as planned. The game is in development and details will change.

2055. The sea has the coasts, and every stretch of water belongs to someone: a navy, a pirate crew, a neutral harbour, or nobody at all. Who holds the water decides who stops you, who protects you and who you pay.

See it on the world map

Home

ARI home waters
Large and settled: the water round San Diego, safest at the centre and patrolled by ARI cutters.

Neutral harbours

Fishing Federation
Neutral anchorage. The whole sector is a sanctuary.
Breakwater
Neutral. Three rigs joined round one harbour.
The Last Resort
Neutral, off Nassau. The whole sector is a sanctuary.

The free port

Midway, the free port
Midway itself: Meridian's old capital, now a ruin with a survivors' settlement. No government. It answers to nobody, takes anyone's money and asks no questions.

Pirates

Midway's protection ring (Longliners)
Midway has no government, so the settlement pays the Longliners for protection, and they sit here. Pay their fee and you pass. Refuse and you can still deliver to Midway, but you have to get past them; make it and the pay is good.
The Tollmen
Hold the Cut, the drowned gap between the Pacific and the Caribbean. They don't rob you, they bill you. Orderly, uniformed in their own way, and they keep ledgers.
The Driftmark
Hold the drowned Suez cut between the Mediterranean and the Gulf. Pilots and wreckers: pay and they guide you through the wrecks, refuse and the wrecks get one more.
Saltrot
Squat the dead navy ports of Japan and Korea, the Ash Coast. The cruel ones: tolls change with their mood, and they strip boats that argue.
Crew water
A pirate crew: several boats working from one captured rig, with their own painted tag.

Navies

US Atlantic Fleet
Norfolk's water. Calls ARI's people deserters; boards and fines runners.
Royal Navy (Portsmouth)
Portsmouth's water. By the book.
Russia (Severomorsk)
Severomorsk's water, and the Atlantic end of the Melt. Sells passage, not friendship.
China (Sanya)
Sanya's water. Open for business to everyone, and counts everything.
Iran (Bandar Abbas)
Bandar Abbas and the strait. Everything has a price.

Open water

Open sea
Nobody holds it. Raiders, weather, and no help.

Dex's Wake

Dex's Wake picket
The outer edge of the drone water. Drones patrol out this far: survivable at speed, deadly if you stop.
Dex's Wake
Water full of leftover war drones that attack anything. Closed in practice: nothing comes back.

Closed

Radiation, closed
Closed for ever. Never sailable.
