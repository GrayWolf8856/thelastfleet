Lantern rigs

The world of Runners, as planned. The game is in development and details will change.

People live here. They do not trade and they do not attack. Lit at night.

A lantern rig is an old oil rig that people have made into a home: homes built from shipping containers stacked up to ten high, bridges between them, a farm and fish tanks in the middle, a water tower and windmills on top, and balconies over the water. There are 90 of them on the chart, out in the open sea and in the crews' water where nobody else would settle. Each is one of ten designs. From a distance at night, they are the only lights on the sea.

Find them on the world map (Show: Rigs, Lantern rigs)

Four Towers

A container tower on each corner, five to ten high, bridges between them at different levels, a courtyard farm with fish tanks and a greenhouse in the middle; a water tower and two windmills up top.

Horseshoe

Homes in a U round three sides, open to the south and the sun, a bridge across the open end, the farm and greenhouse inside the U, washing strung across the yard.

The Wall

One great wall of homes seven to ten high along the windward side, its balconies over the sea and over the gardens in its lee; polytunnels, fish tanks and a yard under tarps below it; windmills and a water tower along its top.

Twin Spires

Two towers nine and ten boxes high, three bridges between them, a low block of homes on the south edge; the farm, fish tanks and a greenhouse between the towers.

The Terraces

Homes stepping up from one box at the south edge to nine at the north, a garden on every step; the farm and fish tanks alongside; a water tower at the summit and a windmill.

The Ring

An unbroken ring of homes three to six high round the deck's edge, balconies over the sea outside and the courtyard farm inside, bridges crossing the courtyard both ways.

Scatter

Seven small towers of every height set down wherever they would fit, a web of bridges between them, beds and tanks in the gaps.

Lighthouse

One tall tower in the middle, nine and ten high with a water tower and a lamp on top, the farm all round its foot, low rows of homes on the north and south edges.

The Fishponds

Homes in an L along the north and west, five to eight high; the rest of the deck fish tanks and grow beds, a greenhouse, the boats' people's washing.

Windward

A long block of homes six to nine high on the weather side carrying three windmills, solar on every other roof, a low row to the south; rain catchers and beds between them.
