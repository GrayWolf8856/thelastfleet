Saltrot

The world of Runners, as planned. The game is in development and details will change.

A large pirate faction. They hold the Ash Coast, in the Ash Sea.

HullUpperworksAccent

Logo and colours: draft art.

Ragged, sick and fearless. They've worked hot water so long they've stopped caring. There's no dose meter in this world, and they don't want one.

How they live
Hot salvage from the dead ports.
What they sell
Hot salvage (the most valuable salvage in the world, marked hot); clean tech stripped from Yokosuka and Busan (radar sets, chips, fire-control boards); the Ash Coast toll (cargo, sometimes crew).
Their seat
The Yokosuka hulks
Who wants them gone
China (Sanya), ARI and the Fishing Federation. Saltrot raids the North Pacific routes.
How to know their ships
Hulls left to rust on purpose, white salt streaks down from every scupper, dirty-white upperworks, yellow hazard triangles, and the Broken Trefoil sprayed on bow and wheelhouse.
