The Driftmark

The world of Runners, as planned. The game is in development and details will change.

A large pirate faction. They hold the Suez Cut, in the Choked Sea.

HullUpperworksAccent

Logo and colours: draft art.

Proud pilots. They see themselves as harbour men, not thieves, but they will let a ship that refuses a pilot tear its hull open on a wreck.

How they live
Pilot fees through the wreck field. Refuse and you run it blind.
What they sell
Pilot fees; city salvage from drowned Alexandria, Tel Aviv, Suez and Basra; their channel charts of the Suez Cut, the only true ones, sold at a high price; seized cargo off ships that ran the field without a pilot.
Their seat
Lockgate
Who wants them gone
The Royal Navy (Portsmouth), Iran (Bandar Abbas) and ARI. The Cut is their short road.
How to know their ships
Sand-khaki hulls with dull grey upperworks, yellow-and-black danger-buoy bands at bow and stern, and the Mark sprayed on bow and wheelhouse.
