The Tollmen

The world of Runners, as planned. The game is in development and details will change.

A large pirate faction. They hold the Cut, in the Narrow Seas.

HullUpperworksAccent

Logo and colours: draft art.

Neat, polite, never in a hurry. They write everything down. Debt is their whole language, because the war was the Debt War.

How they live
Tolls on the Cut. They keep ledgers on everyone.
What they sell
Seized cargo of every kind (food, fuel, chips, meds); their ledgers (debts, routes and names, sold as intel); Tollmen passes, a paper that lets a ship through the Cut for a season.
Their seat
The Counting House, old Veracruz
Who wants them gone
Breakwater, the Fishing Federation, US Atlantic Fleet (Norfolk) and ARI. The Cut chokes all their trade.
How to know their ships
Dark navy-black hulls with off-white upperworks, a red-and-white barrier stripe at the deck line, and the Barrier sprayed on bow and wheelhouse.
