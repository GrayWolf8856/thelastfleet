The Flotilla

The world of Runners, as planned. The game is in development and details will change.

A large pirate faction. They roam the Long Fetch, the open South Atlantic, outside every nation's water, and hold no rigs.

HullUpperworksAccent

Logo and colours: draft art.

Organised like a merchant navy. Everyone has a convoy number and a job. They call it taxation, not theft, and they keep a register.

How they live
They carry their own fuel and food and trade at sea. They tax whoever they meet.
What they sell
Fuel and water sold at sea, at a high price; a share of every cargo they meet; repaired and resold ships; passage: they carry people who need to cross the South Atlantic.
Their base
The Convoy, a moving base with no fixed position: a moving town of haulers that never stops, round an old liner.
Who wants them gone
US Atlantic Fleet (Norfolk), Breakwater and ARI. The Convoy sits across the back road to the Cape.
How to know their ships
Every hull the same sand-grey, with a huge black convoy number on both bows and the Line sprayed on bow and wheelhouse.
