Deadweight

The world of Runners, as planned. The game is in development and details will change.

A large pirate faction. They roam the open Indian Ocean, north of Dex's Wake, outside every nation's water, and hold no rigs.

HullUpperworksAccent

Logo and colours: draft art.

Businessmen. They talk about "stock" and "price" and never about theft. They'd rather take a tanker whole than sink one.

How they live
They take crude tankers out of the Gulf. In a sea with no fuel they are the only fuel, at their price.
What they sell
Stolen Gulf crude; fuel sold at sea; captured tankers, resold; ransom: the tanker's owner can buy the ship back.
Their base
The Tank Line, moored in the open Indian Ocean where the marker is: captured tankers moored together, full of stolen Gulf crude.
Who wants them gone
Iran (Bandar Abbas), ARI and US Atlantic Fleet (Norfolk). Gulf crude is the oil half the world wants.
How to know their ships
Black hulls with oil stains running down from the deck edge, dark grey upperworks, a red waterline stripe, and the Load Line sprayed big and red on the side.
