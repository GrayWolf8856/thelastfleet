Ships

The world of Runners, as planned. The game is in development and details will change.

Thirty-eight ships, in five classes. A runner starts in the Scrapper and earns the rest. Each port's yard sells its own ships, so better ships are a reason to travel.

Hunter

Warships and fast armed boats. Hunters take the bounties on the wanted boards, clear pirate crews and their bases, and escort convoys and fishing fleets. Any ship may take a hunting job, but without the guns it is a bad idea, and Relic will say so.

  • DartLevel 1 · A light raider, quick on the water.
  • GunrunnerLevel 1 · The first gunboat: tough and well armed for her size.
  • Scout skiffLevel 1 · The cheapest armed boat: scouting and running away.
  • Armoured runnerLevel 2 · A fast boat with armour: hits, takes a hit and gets out.
  • Patrol EscortLevel 2 · The first proper warship: a convoy escort.
  • SkipjackLevel 2 · A fast, shallow-draft interceptor.
  • Blockade RunnerLevel 3 · Built to break through a blockade.
  • CorvetteLevel 3 · An all-round warship with a hold.
  • Rocket bargeLevel 3 · A slow floating battery.
  • DestroyerLevel 4 · A gun ship.
  • FrigateLevel 4 · A fleet escort.
  • Armed linerLevel 5 · A people carrier turned warship.
  • BattleshipLevel 5 · The heaviest warship there is.
  • Missile cruiserLevel 5 · The missile ship.

Hauler

Container ships, delivery boats and tankers: the ships that carry the world's cargo between rigs and ports.

  • MuleLevel 1 · The first container ship: slow and steady.
  • Supply runnerLevel 1 · A cheap, quick delivery boat.
  • Landing craftLevel 2 · Lands cargo on any shore.
  • WorkhorseLevel 2 · The standard box carrier.
  • FreighterLevel 3 · A long-haul container ship.
  • Refitted tankerLevel 3 · The first tanker.
  • Fleet tankerLevel 4 · The fleet's refuelling ship.
  • LeviathanLevel 4 · Carries more containers than any other ship.

Scavenger

Salvage boats and tugs. Only Scavengers tow. Each step up the ladder opens a new kind of salvage, and at each level there are two different ships to choose between.

How scavenging works: the ladder

The magnet crane lifts salvage onto the deck: grade 1 lifts loose scrap, grade 2 heavy single pieces such as machinery and hull plate, grade 3 the long cable for deep wrecks. Loose scrap goes in the scrap bin; from Level 2 a compactor presses it into pallets that sell for more. Valuable finds go in containers, safe from the weather. The tow winch brings a whole wreck home, worth more than its scrap, but you cannot run while towing.

LevelThe choice
0Scrapper: magnet and a small bin. Drift scrap. She cannot tow.
1Salvage launch for more scrap, or Recovery Workboat for towing and a first container.
2Salvage tender for machinery from the destroyed rigs, or Barge-Hound for the graveyards and war salvage.
3Atlas for the biggest tows and deep wrecks, or Salvage dredger for bulk salvage from the drowned cities.

Wrecks are also called in: over the radio while you sail, and as set jobs on the port boards.

  • ScrapperLevel 0 · The boat every runner starts in. Picks up loose scrap.
  • Recovery WorkboatLevel 1 · The first tug: pulls small wrecks home.
  • Salvage launchLevel 1 · The first scrap boat, with a tow winch for small wrecks.
  • Barge-HoundLevel 2 · A tough scrap boat for working in among wrecks.
  • Salvage tenderLevel 2 · A mid-size tug for medium wrecks.
  • AtlasLevel 3 · The top scavenger: tows the largest wrecks.
  • Salvage dredgerLevel 3 · A slow scrap ship with a huge store. Worth guarding.

Fishing

Only Fishing ships fish. The gear grows with the boat, from rods to the longest net and crab pots.

How fishing works

Find a school of fish, then steer round it, paying the net out behind the boat, until the net wraps the school and the catch comes in. Bigger boats carry a longer net and can close a ring round a bigger school. Schools are found by sonar, by Relic's call-outs, by birds diving or with a bought fishing chart. They move, and they scatter if you come in too fast or too close; a gap left in the ring lets fish escape.

Smuggler

Only Smugglers smuggle: they carry a hidden hold under their honest cargo.

  • WispLevel 1 · Small and plain, with a small hidden compartment.
  • ShadeLevel 2 · A real container sitting on the trap door of a hidden hold.
  • GhostLevel 3 · The top smuggler: real and fake containers over a hidden hold.
