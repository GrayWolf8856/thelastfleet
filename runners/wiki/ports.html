Ports

The world of Runners, as planned. The game is in development and details will change.

Where a runner can dock, what each port makes and needs, and which ships its yard sells. Every port has pictures and more on the world map.

ARI home port

San Diego · held by ARI

Ships sold here
Scrapper, Scout skiff, Dart, Missile cruiser, Recovery Workboat, Salvage launch, Mule, Supply runner, Crude tanker
Exports
Rig parts: pumps, drill heads, valves. ARI is the one place that still makes them.
Needs
Gulf crude from Iran, ship steel from Sanya.
How the door works
Home. Always open.

Fishing Federation anchorage

Off Cabo San Lucas · held by The Fishing Federation

Ships sold here
Skipjack, Fishing boat, Trawler, Seiner, Factory trawler
Exports
Salt fish: food that keeps for a long crossing.
Needs
Fuel, and nets and line from the Royal Navy's stores.

Ships sold here
Armed liner
Exports

Needs

Midway

Midway · held by Nobody

Ships sold here
Wisp, Ghost, Armoured runner
Exports
Anything, no questions. Cargo taken on the toll lines ends up here.
Needs
Medicine and food. Runs that get past the protection ring pay the most.
How the door works
Open to anyone who gets there.

Norfolk

Norfolk, Virginia · held by US Atlantic Fleet

Ships sold here
Patrol Escort, Blockade Runner, Destroyer, Missile cruiser
Exports
Heavy ordnance and missile defence.
Needs
Gulf crude from Iran: the long haul nobody else will make for them.
How the door works
Hardest door on the map for an ARI ship. Standing first, then papers.

Portsmouth

Portsmouth, England · held by Royal Navy

Ships sold here
Corvette, Frigate, Fleet tanker
Exports
Admiralty charts: weather routing and sounding charts. They make storms and shallows readable.
Needs
Radar parts from the Ash Sea's dead ports; ordnance from Norfolk.
How the door works
By the book. Fair to anyone with clean papers.

Severomorsk

Severomorsk, Kola Peninsula · held by Russian Northern Fleet

Ships sold here
Battleship, Crab boat
Exports
Ice pilots and cold-forged hull plate.
Needs
Salt fish from the Federation and medicine from the Last Resort: both from the far side of the world.
How the door works
Pay the pilot and stay in the swept channel. Leave it and you are a target.
