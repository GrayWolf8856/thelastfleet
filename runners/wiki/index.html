Runners wiki

The world of Runners, as planned. The game is in development and details will change.

2055. Ten years after the war, the sea has the coasts. A runner with a small boat hauls cargo between oil rigs and ports, salvages, fishes, hunts or smuggles, and every stretch of water belongs to someone. This wiki holds what is known about that world: its ships, its pirates, its ports and the people who live out on the rigs.
